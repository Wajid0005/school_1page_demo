Our Story

A Legacy of Learning
Since 1998

Founded in 1998, Vidyasagar International School has grown into one of Kolkata's most respected ICSE institutions. We blend rigorous academics with holistic development — shaping students who think critically, lead confidently, and contribute meaningfully to the world.

Our CISCE-recognized curriculum, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and a faculty of over 120 dedicated educators make VIS the first choice for discerning families across West Bengal.

  • ICSE Affiliated, CISCE Recognized Affiliated to the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations
  • Fully Air-Conditioned Smart Classrooms Technology-integrated learning environments across all grades
  • 100% Top College Placement — Class XII Every graduate placed in a premier institution in 2024
